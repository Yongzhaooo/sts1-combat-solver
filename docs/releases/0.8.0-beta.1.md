# 0.8.0-beta.1

## 中文

- 接入 π 2.6 循环搜索与胜线动作精简。精简须保留胜利、血量、成长、药水与受保护遗物计数，不增加回合数。有限搜索不保证全局最优。
- 修复前后端超时不一致。普通请求后端限时 150 秒，深入请求 600 秒，客户端额外等待 10 秒。面板显示实际搜索阶段、模拟次数与循环展开数。
- 自动战斗在允许逃跑的 T1 开局先做无药预检，默认预算 12,000 次。找到战损小于 15 HP 的胜线则保留烟雾弹，否则立即逃跑。预算与阈值支持配置。仅当死亡收割已经上手时，允许即时力量加死亡收割或直接死亡收割后逃跑，按实际回血结算。手动面板也禁止延迟使用烟雾弹。
- 混沌药水比较先使用其他获准药水腾出槽位、再补满空槽的路线。白兽雕像（白象雕像）在战后允许掉药且存在空槽时降低用药保留成本，添水和禁止掉药的场景除外。
- F6 默认附带本局最近五场已结束战斗和当前战斗的实际出牌、选牌与手牌顺序，无需测试模式。记录仅留本机，导出后由玩家自行分享。
- 修复搜索轨迹失控、地精小队角色识别、选牌身份及部分动作结算问题。共用搜索与 Mod 适配层分离，两平台共用运行文件清单。

验证：本次 22 项检查通过。27 个可回放局面与 π 2.6 动作和资源结果一致，保留 23 场胜利。此前共享内核的 96 个固定局面保留 75 场胜利。药水边界、真实循环进度与超时协议另有定向检查。这些不等于实机整局验收，也不保证所有精简路线后续 RNG 不变。

燃烧契约中途选牌导入与换选调序继续暂缓，待静默猎手求解器工作时重新评估。冻结的采集和训练运行包保持不变。

感谢七月流火（聊天昵称：七月火）、૭٩奶油糖果、幼猫（浪花）提供局面、出牌对照与异常反馈，感谢严肃研究鸡煲打法中提供选牌和循环思路。

## English

- Adopts π 2.6 loop search and winning-route reduction. Reduction must preserve victory, HP, growth, potions and protected relic counters without adding turns. Bounded search does not guarantee optimal play.
- Coordinates backend and client timeouts: 150 seconds for normal requests, 600 for deep requests, plus 10 seconds of client grace. The panel shows actual search stages, simulations and loop transitions.
- At an untouched, escapable turn-one opening, automatic combat runs a potion-free probe, defaulting to 12,000 simulations. It keeps Smoke Bomb only if a winning route loses less than 15 HP; otherwise it escapes immediately. Both settings are configurable. Reaper already in hand permits an immediate-strength/Reaper or Reaper-only prefix, with actual healing recorded. Manual recommendations also exclude delayed escape.
- Entropic Brew compares using other authorized potions first to free slots. White Beast Statue reduces potion conservation costs when a reward is available and slots are empty, excluding Sozu and blocked rewards.
- F6 includes ordered plays, selections and hand order from the last five completed fights and the current fight by default. Records stay local until exported and shared.
- Fixes runaway search trajectories, Gremlin role identification and selection/action-resolution cases. Shared search is separated from the Mod adapter, with one runtime inventory for both platforms.

Validation: 22 checks passed. The 27 replayable positions matched π 2.6 actions and resources, retaining 23 wins. The earlier shared-core regression retained 75 wins across 96 positions. Targeted checks cover potion boundaries, live loop progress and timeout messages. Full live-game runs remain untested; reduced routes can change subsequent RNG.

Burning Pact mid-selection import and reselection ordering remain deferred until Silent solver work. Frozen collection and training packages are unchanged.
